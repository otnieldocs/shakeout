## How to sign

Open the pull request, then post the following as a comment on it, filling in your
details:

```
I have read the Shakeout CLA and I hereby sign it.

Full name:      <your legal name>
GitHub username: <your username>
Email:          <your email>
Date:           <YYYY-MM-DD>
```

Signing once covers all of your future contributions to this project. You do not need to
repeat it on subsequent pull requests.

If you are contributing on behalf of a company and clause 4 applies to you, contact
**otniel@kreasistudio.co.id** first so a Corporate CLA can be put in place.
